Archive

The archive is its own library. Titles moved there leave their library on disk and stop showing up next to everything else, but stay one button away on clients that support it, such as Moonfin. Make a library for the archive first (Dashboard → Libraries), then pick it here.

Libraries

Who may see the archive

The archive library is opened to the people above and closed to everybody else, in every app. Somebody on "all libraries" who may not see it is given every other library instead, including libraries added later, and gets "all libraries" back once they're allowed. Turn this off to manage library access by hand.
